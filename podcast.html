  1. What does an ADHD burnout feel like?

    Nov 6, 2026 · 7 min

    Your boss praised your presentation, and that night you were sick to your stomach again. Alex and Jordan talk about a side of ADHD burnout that coworkers never see: the same work output takes far more energy from a worker with ADHD than it takes from the person at the next desk. They talk about the body signs workers describe, stomach trouble, migraines, infections that keep coming back, and anger at tiny things. They talk about why a vacation doesn't fix it, and they go through a three-part fix that changes the job itself: find the tasks that use the most energy, change one part of the structure, and test whether a normal workday needs panic to get through it. Plus a note on talking to a professional you trust.

About the Burnout to Balance Podcast

Sick of workplace drama, impossible expectations, toxic bosses, and dreading another day at work? You're in the right place. Burnout to Balance takes an unfiltered look at workplace burnout, dysfunctional management, difficult coworkers, chronic stress, and the psychological toll of working in an environment that drains you.

The podcast is based on the book Burnout to Balance: Your 8-Step Guide to Thriving in a Messy Workplace, written by Andrea Gomez, an ICU nurse with 27 years in critical care. It brings workplace psychology and lived experience together. No motivational speeches. No pretending a positive attitude will fix poor management. Just an honest look at why people burn out, how toxic workplaces operate, and what you can do to protect your mental health without sacrificing your career.

New episodes are short, usually under ten minutes, and you can play every one of them on this page. Not sure if what you're feeling is burnout? Take the free seven-question burnout check, or join the newsletter for new writing on surviving a messy workplace.
